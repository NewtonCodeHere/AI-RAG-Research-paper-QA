Welcome to AI Research Paper Assistant

A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platform designed for researchers, students, and engineers.

1 Upload: Drop research paper PDFs to extract text and generate sentence-transformer embeddings.
2 Index: Chunks are stored in ChromaDB vector space with page-level metadata.
3 Query: Ask questions to retrieve grounded context and receive Gemini-synthesized answers with exact page references.
Answers are retrieved from ChromaDB vector space and synthesized by Google Gemini with verified page citations.
